## Main post — #showcase / #share-your-build

**Lumen — medicine, made legible** 🩵

Two things in an ordinary medicine cabinet — one prescription, one over-the-counter
sleep aid — can stack up the same drying and drowsiness effects. No single label calls
it an interaction, so most checkers miss it completely.

I built something that catches it, and shows you the exact FDA sentence that warns
about it.

**Lumen does three things:**
📷 Photograph your medicine boxes → what each one is for, and which combinations to ask
a pharmacist about
📄 Paste your lab results → every value in plain language, including how many were
completely normal
🩺 Describe a symptom → how urgently to seek care, what would change that, and where
the nearest real ER actually is

Every interaction claim is quoted **word for word from the official FDA label**, with
the drug named, so you can check it yourself. It never diagnoses you, and it stores
nothing.

🔗 https://lumen-virid-sigma.vercel.app

**The ask:** try one thing and tell me where it breaks. Paste an old lab report, or
type in whatever's actually in your medicine cabinet. I especially want to hear about
a medication it couldn't find, or an explanation that felt off.

Happy to return the favour — drop your link and I'll give you real feedback, not just
a 👍
